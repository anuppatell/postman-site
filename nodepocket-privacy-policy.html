Node Pocket on-device

Privacy Policy

Node Pocket does not collect your personal data. Your code, your projects and your databases stay on your device.

no accounts no analytics no tracking effective 9 Sep 2026

Node Pocket is a JavaScript development environment for iPhone and iPad. It runs a Node.js runtime, a package installer, a git client, a web preview and a database entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

The short version. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. We operate no servers that receive your data. Everything you write stays on your device unless you deliberately send it somewhere — for example by cloning or pushing to a git repository you control.

01 Who we are

Node Pocket is made by Elevarelab ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, contact support@elevarelab.site.

Where data protection law applies, we are the data controller for the very limited processing described here.

02 What we do not collect

We want to be specific, because "we value your privacy" means nothing on its own.

No user accounts

You never register, sign in, or give us an email address.

No analytics or telemetry

The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK of any kind. We do not measure feature usage, sessions or retention.

No advertising, no tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites, and we share nothing with data brokers or advertising networks.

No servers of ours

There is no backend for your app to talk to. There is nowhere for your data to reach us, and we could not read your projects even if asked to.

Our App Store privacy declaration states "Data Not Collected" across every category, and the app ships an Apple privacy manifest declaring no collected data types and no tracking.

03 What stays on your device

Your projects

Source files, folders, and anything you create, import or clone.

Installed packages

The contents of node_modules in each project.

Your databases

Data written by apps you run through the built-in MongoDB-compatible database, stored as files in the app's Documents folder.

Your settings

Appearance, editor theme, font size and similar preferences.

Editor state

Breakpoints, recently opened files and console history.

Because these files live in the app's Documents folder, they are included in your iPhone or iPad backups if you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup. Those backups are made and protected by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us. Installed node_modules folders are deliberately excluded from backup to save your storage; they can be reinstalled at any time.

04 Network connections

Node Pocket works offline. It connects to the internet only for the actions below, and only when you trigger them. In each case you are connecting directly to that third party — nothing passes through us.

outbound requests

You search for or install an npm package

registry.npmjs.org

The package name and version you asked for. As with any web request, your IP address is visible to the recipient.

You preview a project that imports packages in the browser, including the built-in React templates

esm.sh

The name and version of the module being loaded.

You clone, browse, pull from or push to GitHub

github.com · api.github.com

The repository you asked for, the content you chose to push, and — if you have added a personal access token for that host — that token, as authentication.

You clone from, or push to, any other git host

the URL you entered

The repository data and, if stored for that host, your access token.

Code you write makes a network request

wherever your code points

Whatever your code sends. You are the developer; the app does not inspect, filter or log this.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy, and their handling of your request is governed by it, not by this policy.

05 Access tokens

If you add a personal access token so you can use a private repository, it is stored in the iOS Keychain, the system's encrypted credential store, filed against the specific host it belongs to.

It is sent only to the host it was stored for. A token for one git host is never sent to another.

It is never written to logs, the console, exported projects or diagnostics.

It never reaches us. We have no server and no ability to read it.

You can remove it in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes it.

06 Permissions
  • Local network. Projects you run can start a web server on the device. iOS may ask for permission the first time a server is reachable on your local network. You can decline; the app's own preview still works.
  • Files. If you import or export a project, iOS shows its own file picker. The app receives only the folder you choose.

Node Pocket does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or health data, and contains no code that would use them.

07 Information Apple may provide

If you choose to share diagnostics with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us aggregated crash reports and performance statistics. These come from Apple, are aggregated, and are not linked to your identity by us. If you have opted out with Apple, we receive nothing. Apple also gives us anonymous, aggregated sales and download figures. We do not attempt to identify individual users from any of this.

08 Children

Node Pocket is a developer tool intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

09 Your rights and choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. In practical terms:

  • To delete your data, delete the projects and databases inside the app, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored including any saved tokens.
  • To stop all network activity, use the app offline. Only the actions in section 04 need a connection.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a jurisdiction with comparable law such as California, you have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. You are welcome to exercise them by writing to us; our answer will be that we hold no personal data about you, and we will say so in writing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

10 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11 Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything in this policy, go to support@elevarelab.site. We answer privacy questions directly.
